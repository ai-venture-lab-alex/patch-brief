# Turn your gameplay clip into a Short

Use your own footage. Fill in the brackets before recording. Keep plot details out if your channel is spoiler-free.

| Beat | Footage | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4 sec | Start at the visible outcome | “Here’s what happens when I try [action] in GTA VI.” |
| 4–12 sec | Show the setup | “I started at [spoiler-free location or landmark]. The goal was [observable goal].” |
| 12–24 sec | Show what changed | “Watch [visible detail]. In this clip, [describe what changed].” |
| 24–30 sec | Replay the key beat | “Would you try [alternative]? I’ll compare it in another clip.” |

**Caption:** Testing [action] on [console]. Here’s what happened in this run.

Keep claims tied to the footage. Mention your platform or settings if they affect what viewers see.

## See the difference in the first four seconds

Example for a clip from your own stream, using a fictional driving challenge.

- **Raw opening:** Four seconds of driving toward a corner with no context.
- **Result-first opening:** Show the near miss. Say, “One try. I cleared the corner without braking.”
- **Question-first opening:** Freeze on the approach. Say, “Can I clear this corner without braking?” Cut right into the attempt.

Pick the version that makes sense to someone who missed the stream. Keep the rest of the clip the same when comparing the two cuts. Check whether viewers stay past the opening if you have retention data.

## Worked example for a driving clip

Illustrative footage and wording. Replace the actions and outcome with what your clip actually shows.

| Time | Picture | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4 sec | Show the car clearing the wall | “One try. I cleared the corner without braking.” |
| 4–12 sec | Replay the approach | “I started wide so I could turn in late. Watch the front wheel as it reaches the curb.” |
| 12–24 sec | Hold the full corner through the exit | “I let off the throttle before turning. The car drifted toward the outside wall, then straightened when I eased back onto the throttle.” |
| 24–30 sec | Replay the closest frame to the wall | “Would the same line work in the rain? That’s my next test.” |

**Caption:** One corner, one attempt. Watch how close I get to the outside wall.
