# Turn your gameplay clip into a Short

Use your own footage. Fill in the brackets before recording. Keep plot details out if your channel is spoiler-free.

| Beat | Footage | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4 sec | Start at the visible outcome | “Here’s what happens when I try [action] in GTA VI.” |
| 4–12 sec | Show the setup | “I started at [spoiler-free location or landmark]. The goal was [observable goal].” |
| 12–24 sec | Show what changed | “Watch [visible detail]. In this clip, [describe what changed].” |
| 24–30 sec | Replay the key beat | “Would you try [alternative]? I’ll compare it in another clip.” |

**Caption:** Testing [action] on [console]. Here’s what happened in this run.

Keep claims tied to the footage. Mention your platform or settings if they affect what viewers see.
